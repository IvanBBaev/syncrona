- The native Fluent compiler supports `CrossScopePrivilege()` (`sys_scope_privilege`), `UserPreference()` (`sys_user_preference`), `ApplicationMenu()` (`sys_app_application`) and `UiPolicy()` (`sys_ui_policy` with its `sys_ui_policy_action` and `sys_ui_policy_rl_action` rows), byte for byte as SDK 4.13.6 writes them, with the SDK's validations and messages.
