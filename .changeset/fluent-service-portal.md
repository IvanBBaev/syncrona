- The native Fluent compiler supports `SPAngularProvider()`, `CssInclude()`, `JsInclude()`, `SPWidgetDependency()`, `SPTheme()` and `SPPageRouteMap()` (with their m2m include and provider rows), byte for byte as SDK 4.13.6 writes them.
- The native Fluent compiler supports `SPWidget()` and `SPHeaderFooter()` (with their templates and dependency and angular provider rows), byte for byte as SDK 4.13.6 writes them.
- The native Fluent compiler supports `SPPage()` (with its containers, rows, columns and widget instances) and `SPMenu()` (with its menu items), byte for byte as SDK 4.13.6 writes them.
- The native Fluent compiler supports `ServicePortal()` (with its search source, knowledge base, catalog and taxonomy rows), byte for byte as SDK 4.13.6 writes them.
